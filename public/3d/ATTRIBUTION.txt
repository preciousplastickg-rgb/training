ATTRIBUTION — exercise-anim muscle textures
===========================================

The muscle relief, zone and fibre maps of the twin are derived from Z-Anatomy.

Source
------
"Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy" — CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
  Authors: Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy), Marcin Zielinski (Blender add-on),

"BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science" — CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  Original model: Kousaku Okubo
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html

What was used and how it was changed
------------------------------------
- 128 superficial skeletal muscle meshes (64 per side) and 24 bone meshes used only as
  landmarks, from Z-Anatomy Startup.blend (downloaded 2026-11-08).
- The muscles were scaled to the twin's stature, warped per body segment to the twin's
  joint landmarks, shrink-wrapped to the twin's skin and baked into 2048x2048 UV maps.
- Derived files (licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, ShareAlike), app assets in dist/:
    dist/twin.glb           — normal map embedded in material Twin_App_glTF
    dist/twin_id.webp       — muscle zone ID map
    dist/twin_idsoft.webp   — soft zone-edge mask
    dist/twin_fibre.webp    — muscle fibre texture (from the fibre direction map)
    dist/muscles.json       — zone table and muscle lists
    dist/ex/*_start.webp, *_end.webp — stills with the muscle highlight
  Working copies: renders/twin_normal.png, twin_id.png, twin_idsoft.png, twin_fibre.png, twin_flow.png,
  bench.blend (scene "Anatomy").

Attribution line for the app (e.g. an About screen):
  Muscle anatomy: Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0), based on BodyParts3D, The Database Center
  for Life Science (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP).
